# STAR Narrative

## Situation
Student loan debt is becoming a major problem for college students in the United States. As tuition costs starts to increase,
more students are required to take student loans. What used to be an opportunity turned out to become a burden for every college student. The amount of student loan debt exceeded over 1.7 trillion dollars. If the problem isn't mitigated in time then future college students would suffer. It is only a matter of time to speak out about this situation and spread awareness to people of these demographics: college students, college graduates, college dropouts, high school students, and high school graduates.

## Task
The task is to understand how long it takes for a college student to pay of their student loans. The viewer should be able to understand the reality of how long a college student takes to pay off their tuition. They should also be aware about the consequences of taking a student loan and be able to spread the word to other people.

## Action
I built a website using React and Reacharts and cleaned/transformed into into a format that can be used for charting. There are two interactive charts that the viewer can react to and it explains the student loan problem.
The first visualization is a line chart that shows the average student loan balance from 2007 to 2025. Users can hover over the chart to see the exact balance for each year. This interaction allows viewers to closely examine how the debt changes over time.

The second visualization shows the total student loan debt in the United States over the same time period. This chart provides a broader perspective by showing how the overall national debt has grown alongside the average balance per borrower.

## Result
The visualizations show a clear upward trend in student loan debt. For example, the average student loan balance increased from $18,230 in 2007 to $39,550 in 2025. This increase highlights how much the financial burden of higher education has grown.

When interacting with the chart, users can hover over different years to see the exact balance and observe how the debt steadily rises over time.

One limitation of this visualization is that it focuses only on national averages and totals. It does not break the data down by state, demographics of the borrower, or borrower income level, which could provide additional insights into how student debt affects different groups.
